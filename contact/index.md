Phase Zero

## What the engagement involves

What you send

Historical sessions with appointment counts by type, a complexity flag, the site, the provider, the half-day, and realized technician minutes. Patient identifiers stay in your systems. How the export is prepared, including whether it is protected health information in your hands, is agreed with your privacy office before anything moves.

What you get back

A model retrained on your history, its error against the fixed ratio and the historical mean, interval coverage, the subspecialties it handles worst, and a recommendation to continue or to stop. If it does not beat your current rule, that is the finding, and it is yours to keep.

Production use with protected health information is a later step, under a business associate agreement and a security review.

## Tell us about your staffing question

For COOs, practice administrators and clinical operations leaders. A first enquiry needs only your site count, current staffing rule and the question you want to answer. Please do not include patient information or identifiable staff records.

What to expect

- A reply from the team within 2 business days.
- We will ask a few follow-up questions to understand your session volume and the scheduling rule you use today.
- If Phase Zero fits, we will propose a scoped diagnostic. If it does not, we will say so.

Phase Zero in brief

A fixed-fee engagement. We train a forecast model on your historical sessions and compare its error against your current rule. For this step, data handling is agreed with your privacy office before anything moves. If the model does not beat your rule, that finding is yours and the engagement ends.
