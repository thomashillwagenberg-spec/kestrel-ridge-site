Contact

Ask about Phase Zero.

Phase Zero is a fixed fee starting at $7,500. It runs on de-identified operational data you already hold: per-session counts by appointment type, complexity flags and realized technician minutes, with no patient identifiers leaving your clinic. The first conversation is about what you can export, who owns the decision to stop, and whether a forecast beats the ratio you use today.

Send a message

If it is useful, say how many providers and sites you run, and what your current staffing rule is.

This opens the message in your own mail client with the fields filled in. Nothing is sent, stored or transmitted until you press send there. This site has no server to receive a form and no third party is involved. You can also write directly to hello@kestrelridgehealth.com.

What we will ask for

Historical sessions with appointment counts by type, a complexity flag, the site, the provider, the half-day, and realized technician minutes. No patient identifier, name, date of birth, diagnosis, medication or note, because the system has nowhere to put them.

What we will tell you first

That this system has only ever run on synthetic data, that no outcome has been measured anywhere, and that Phase Zero of a pilot is designed to end the engagement if a model trained on your history does not beat your current rule.

Privacy on this site

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts and no embedded fonts from anyone else. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent to. This page is a static file.
