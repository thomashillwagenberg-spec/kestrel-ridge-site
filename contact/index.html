Ask about Phase Zero.

Phase Zero is a fixed fee starting at $7,500. You send an operational export. You get back a forecast trained on your own history, scored against the ratio you use today, and a written recommendation on whether to go further.

What the engagement involves

What you send

Historical sessions with appointment counts by type, a complexity flag, the site, the provider, the half-day, and realized technician minutes. Patient identifiers stay in your systems. How the export is prepared, including whether it is protected health information in your hands, is agreed with your privacy office before anything moves.

What you get back

A model retrained on your history, its error against the fixed ratio and the historical mean, interval coverage, the subspecialties it handles worst, and a recommendation to continue or to stop. If it does not beat your current rule, that is the finding, and it is yours to keep.

Production use with protected health information is a later step, under a business associate agreement and a security review.

Send a message

Useful in a first message: how many providers and sites you run, the staffing rule you use today, and how far back your session history goes.

This opens the message in your own mail client with the fields filled in. Nothing is sent, stored or transmitted until you press send there. This site has no server to receive a form and no third party is involved. You can also write directly to hello@kestrelridgehealth.com.

Privacy on this site

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts and no embedded fonts from anyone else. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent to. This page is a static file.
