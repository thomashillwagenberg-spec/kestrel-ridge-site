What this check is, and what it is not

It is

  • A short screening of how exposed one typical session is to technician gaps, based on numbers you enter.
  • Rule-based and transparent. Every band comes from a fixed rule shown on this page and on the worksheet.
  • Private by design. Answers stay in your browser unless you choose to send a summary.

It is not

  • Not a benchmark. The cut-offs are screening rules written by Kestrel Ridge Health, not industry norms.
  • Not a forecast. It does not use your schedule history. Phase Zero does that.
  • Not clinical advice, and not a staffing ratio recommendation.
  • Not a place for patient information. It only asks for staffing counts.

Common questions

What does the Technician Coverage Risk Check measure?

It looks at six areas: how much one absence removes from a session, call-off frequency, open positions, whether visit mix is part of planning, same-day schedule changes, and when the technician plan is last checked.

How is the overall band worked out?

Each area is scored Low, Elevated or High by a fixed rule. The overall band is High when two or more areas are High, or one is High with two or more Elevated. It is Elevated when one area is High or two or more are Elevated. Otherwise it is Low. At least four areas must be answered.

Do I need to give an email to see results?

No. Results appear on the page as soon as you finish. Sending a summary to Kestrel Ridge Health is optional.
