## What this site collects

This site does not run Cloudflare Web Analytics. We do not add advertising pixels, session recording or a chat widget. Fonts are served from this domain.

## Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is optional. Its script loads only after you choose Allow analytics. It records page views and clicks on links to our contact page. A link click is an expression of interest, not a submitted inquiry. We do not send form contents, names, email addresses, patient information, URL queries or URL fragments through this integration. The referring website’s origin may be sent to help identify traffic sources.

Google receives technical information when your browser connects to its service and uses Analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Our tag disables Google signals and advertising personalization and sets Analytics cookie expiry to 180 days without extending it on each visit. We remember your allow or decline choice in browser local storage for 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page.

You can change your choice using Analytics settings at the bottom of any page. Declining disables further measurement by this integration and removes the Analytics cookies accessible to this site. It does not remove information already received by Google. We review aggregate reports to improve useful content and navigation. Google processes Analytics information under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Our voluntary reader ratings are described separately in this notice.

## Optional article ratings

If you choose to rate an article, we send its path, your usefulness rating, an optional fixed improvement choice and a random browser identifier to a Cloudflare Worker. We request no name, email or free-text comment. Please do not submit patient information.

Your browser stores the random identifier and which articles you have rated in local storage. The service hashes the identifier separately for each article to limit repeat ratings. Our Cloudflare D1 database stores that article-specific hash, the rating, improvement choice and submission time. It does not store your IP address or the original browser identifier. Cloudflare may process network information to deliver and protect the service under its own privacy policy.

We use responses to improve articles and report aggregate feedback privately. Rating records are removed after 180 days by a daily cleanup. Browser markers remain until you clear this site’s browser data. Clearing them can allow another response; we do not verify reader identity. Rating an article is optional and is not required to read it.

## The contact form

The contact form asks for your name, organization, email address and message. Submitting it sends those details to our Cloudflare Worker, which uses Resend to deliver the inquiry to our business mailbox. The form reports whether the delivery service accepted it. Please do not submit patient information.

Cloudflare provides the form endpoint and, when configured, Turnstile verification to help prevent automated submissions. Resend processes the submitted details for email delivery.

## The waitlist

The waitlist asks for an email address and nothing else. The button sends that address through our Cloudflare Worker and Resend for delivery to our business mailbox. We use the address to tell you when  Kestrel Ridge Health  opens to new clinics, and we may send you occasional updates about our progress before that time. Ask us to remove it and we will, and we will not use it for anything else.

Any message we send to a waitlist address says who it is from, gives you a way to stop hearing from us, and carries the postal address the law requires of a commercial email. We act on a request to stop promptly, and we do not sell or pass the list to anyone.

## What we do with what you send

We use your name, organization, email address and message to read your inquiry and reply to it, and to carry on the conversation you started. We use a waitlist address to tell you when the product opens to new clinics and may send you occasional updates before that time. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to, we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. There is no advertising technology anywhere in this site or in our contact handling.

If you send us business information in the course of an inquiry, we treat it as confidential to that conversation.
